They never
get tired.

Outrun mode puts a horde on your tail. Ram the walkers, chain combos and keep your fuel up. Wreck the car or let them catch you, and the run is over.

Night falls.
Lights on.

On long runs the sun goes down and the road goes dark. Upgrade your headlights in the garage to throw the beam further down the hill.
